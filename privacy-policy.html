  • To create your account and keep you securely logged in.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies (including secure, HttpOnly session cookies) to keep you signed in and to remember items in your cart and wishlist. We also use analytics cookies to understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some features (such as login and checkout) will not work without them.

4. Payment Processing

Online payments are handled by Razorpay, a third-party payment gateway. When you pay, your payment details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay under their own security standards and privacy policy. We receive only the payment result (such as success/failure and a transaction reference). Please review Razorpay's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with trusted service providers only so that the Service can function:

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — to measure marketing performance. See Meta's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request.

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including HTTPS/SSL encryption, encrypted password storage, and secure session cookies. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
