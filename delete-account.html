Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Muscle Tonik account and the personal data associated with it, for both the Muscle Tonik website (muscletonik.com) and the Muscle Tonik Android app (com.muscletonik.app), developed by Muscle Tonik.

How to request account deletion

We do not currently offer an in-app delete button, so account deletion is handled by our support team on request. To delete your account, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to support@muscletonik.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Account".
  3. In the email, include the email address or mobile number registered to your Muscle Tonik account, so we can verify it is you.
  4. Send the email. For security, we may ask you to confirm the request from the same email/phone on file before we proceed.
We will verify your identity and delete your account within 30 days of a confirmed request, and we will email you once it is done.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your password (already stored only in encrypted form)
  • Your saved shipping and billing addresses
  • Your cart and wishlist
  • Your account profile and login sessions

What data may be kept, and for how long

Some records must be retained even after account deletion, to comply with Indian tax, GST, accounting, and legal obligations, and to prevent fraud:

Data keptWhyRetention period
Order and invoice records (including transaction amount and reference ID)Tax, GST, and accounting lawUp to 8 years, then deleted
Records needed for an open order, refund, or disputeTo complete the transaction or resolve the disputeUntil the matter is resolved, then deleted per the period above

Where records are retained for legal reasons, they are kept securely and are no longer linked to an active account. Payment card / UPI details are never stored by us — those are handled by our payment provider, Razorpay.

Delete some data without deleting your account

If you only want to remove or correct specific information (for example, an old saved address), you can do so from your profile when logged in, or email support@muscletonik.com and we will help.
